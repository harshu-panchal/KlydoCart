1. Executive Summary & Transport Architecture

The KlydoCart Delivery Partner App employs a multi-tiered real-time notification engine designed to guarantee immediate order delivery dispatch, offline push reachability, task queue management, and race-condition-free order acceptance.

1. Real-Time WebSockets (Socket.io)

Delivers sub-second notifications, popups, driver location streaming, and acceptance/rejection responses while the driver is actively using the app.

2. Push Notifications (FCM Admin SDK)

Reaches offline drivers when the device is locked, browser tab closed, or app running in background via high-priority FCM data payloads.

3. In-App Notification Feed (MongoDB)

Stores persistent audit records for system broadcasts, account verification alerts, earnings notifications, and administrative updates.
